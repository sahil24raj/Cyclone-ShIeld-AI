# CycloneShield AI — Model Card: P-CHMVM v2.4

## Model Details
- **Model Name**: CycloneShield Multi-Hazard Exposure & Physical Vulnerability Model (P-CHMVM v2.4)
- **Model Version**: 2.4.0
- **Model Type**: Multi-Criteria Hydrodynamic & Spatial Exposure Engine with Baseline ML Sensitivity Calibration
- **Target Geography**: Coastal Andhra Pradesh, Bay of Bengal (`16.50°N–17.50°N, 81.75°E–82.75°E`)
- **Release Date**: September 2026
- **License**: MIT / Open Observational Research License

---

## Intended Use
- **Primary Use Case**: Rapid multi-hazard risk prioritization and asset triage for disaster management authorities (NDMA, SDMA, District Collectors) in coastal urban and port ecosystems during tropical cyclone emergencies.
- **Decision Support**: Identifies critical hospitals, electrical substations, and evacuation arteries vulnerable to compound wind, surge, and precipitation hazards.

## Out-of-Scope & Non-Intended Use
- **Individual Property Structural Guarantees**: Does not replace professional structural engineering building inspections.
- **Long-Term Climate Projections**: Designed for tactical 24h–72h landfall emergency response, not 50-year climate risk projections.
- **Financial Insurance Underwriting**: Model outputs should not be used for commercial property underwriting without verified post-disaster insurance loss datasets.

## Model Architecture & Physical Formulation

$$\text{Composite Risk} = 0.35 \times \text{Hazard} + 0.25 \times \text{Vulnerability} + 0.25 \times \text{Exposure} + 0.15 \times \text{Criticality}$$

- **Hazard ($H$)**: Weighted combination of normalized wind velocity ($50\%$), 24h rainfall ($35\%$), and storm surge ($15\%$).
- **Vulnerability ($V$)**: Asset archetype physical vulnerability + elevation deficit $- 15 \cdot \text{BackupPower}$.
- **Exposure ($E$)**: $40\% \text{ JRC Water Occurrence} + 35\% \text{ Coastline Proximity} + 25\% \text{ Surge Deficit}$.
- **Criticality ($C$)**: Emergency operational tier (Level-1 Hospitals = 95, Substations = 90, Roads = 40).

## Evaluation & Ethical Limitations

1. **Supervised Target Integrity**: Open-access disaster archives contain macro-event boundaries (DFO FloodArchive), not individual asset building-level structural failure tags. Supervised accuracy metrics are reported as `null` to eliminate circular training fallacies.
2. **Confidence Policy**: Because empirical damage distributions cannot be calibrated without historical post-disaster structural tags, confidence is set to `null` across all prediction objects.
3. **No Synthetic Training**: The model does not train on fake labels generated by random numbers or self-derived risk scores.
